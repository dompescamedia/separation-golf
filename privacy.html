Separation Golf

Privacy

Last updated October 21, 2026.

Separation Golf has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name, email or location.

What stays on your device

Your games, streaks, stats and settings (like Hard mode and team colors) are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser data erases them.

What we count

The game sends anonymous counts to a free counter service (abacus.jasoncameron.dev): for example that a Daily was finished, which score bucket it landed in, and which players were used in between. These counts aren't tied to you or your device, and they power the "how everyone did" numbers on the result screen.

Other services the page loads

  • Fonts from Google Fonts.
  • Player photos from MLB's image server.
  • Links to X, Instagram and Baseball-Reference only open when you tap them.

There are no ads or ad trackers today. If that changes, this page will say so first.
