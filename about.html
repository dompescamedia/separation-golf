Separation Golf

About

Separation Golf is a free daily baseball puzzle. You connect two big leaguers through the teammates they shared, in as few jumps as you can. The shortest route is an eagle, and par is two jumps more.

It's made by Dom Pesca, a fan, as an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Major League Baseball, the MLB Players Association, or any club.

Where the data comes from

  • Rosters from 1871 to 2025: the Lahman Baseball Database.
  • The 2026 season: the MLB Stats API.
  • Career WAR: Baseball-Reference.
